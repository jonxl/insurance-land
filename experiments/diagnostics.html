Insurers

How are insurer portfolios assigned?

Each owner's homes stay together. Existing ownership portfolios are ranked by their total initial property value and allocated to Blue, Green, and Orange using the ownership distribution. Coverage is purchased from the assigned firm when eligible and affordable. The seed fixes the assignment; insurer colors do not imply rank.
