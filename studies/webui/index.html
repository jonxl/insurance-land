The same five checks for “Peace”, “Standard”, and “Chaos”: initial geography, resource allocations, noise and prices, disaster sampling, and recorded outcomes. Switch worlds to compare their explanations and figures.

All worlds share the seed, population, initial cash budget, annual income budget, insurer capital, region sizes, disaster size rule, hidden risk field, insurance terms, and baseline policies. “Chaos” grows tenfold from its first-period rate to its last-period rate. “Standard” defaults to 0.1 events per period; the other worlds average 0.5.

Scope of this study

Parameters are illustrative, not calibrated. Income and rent currently settle each period; the proposed 10/20-run payment clock is unfinished. Agents do not yet receive public disaster history. These are fixed baseline policies, not Q-learning. Period zero is after opening premiums. Sampled statistical agreement supports the numerical tests; it does not prove the model correct or establish causal findings from one seed.
